Wake-Up Stroke — Treatment Guidance
Window Summary (CSBPR Box 5A)
✅ <4.5h from onset/found → TNK window OPEN
⚠️ 4.5–6h → TNK for select patients — advanced imaging (CTP / MRI) required
⚠️ 6–9h → TNK only in discussion with stroke expert, tissue-based selection
⚠️ <24h + LVO → EVT window (>6h requires CTA collaterals or perfusion selection)
🚫 >24h → Outside standard treatment windows
Wake-up stroke: TNK window = time from waking/found. MRI DWI-FLAIR mismatch may allow treatment even if >4.5h.
Door-to-needle target: ≤30 min median (≤60 min in ≥90% of patients). If LVO present, late-TNK workup must NOT delay EVT decisions.
Absolute Contraindications to TNK

Active internal bleeding or hemorrhage on imaging — these are the only absolute contraindications. All other concerns are relative CIs (next screen).

Relative Contraindications

Relative CIs require clinical judgment. YES items will be flagged but do not automatically prevent TNK. Tap each item to expand and see why it matters.
